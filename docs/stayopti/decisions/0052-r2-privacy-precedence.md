## Problem and decision

Five new supported-entry assertions failed before repair: the four reviewed
counterexamples and the complete business-control denomination contrast.
An inapplicable claim added UNKNOWN despite being reported as unconsumed. A
generic room noun was also treated as privacy uncertainty, cancelling a verified
exclusive-use fact. Neither behavior was justified by the retained source.

Only known/conflicting OFFER_SCOPED claims enter fact fusion. Inapplicable claims
remain visible with their reason but cannot cancel specific offer observations.
When no scoped fact exists, those claims still cannot authorize generic fallback
as selected-rate certification. Original UNKNOWN and conditional facts survive.

At the observed-offer boundary only, a bounded list/grammar of neutral English
and Italian room denominations is non-evidence for unit privacy, rather than
an assertion of unknown privacy. Other clauses and unsupported descriptions
retain conservative interpretation. Dedicated UNKNOWN fields remain evidence of
uncertainty. Neutral names establish neither privacy nor room type/capacity.
Exclusive-use facts establish privacy only, never category or comfort.
