# D-0066 R1 — Room evidence versus party sufficiency

Date: 2026-09-18. Authorized by Mattia; evaluation-only. Publication is conditional
on the final candidate checks and work-branch remote readback, never on private
commercial outcomes. Prospectively corrects the pure D-0066 comparison; no
historical capture, checkpoint inventory, authorization or receipt is migrated.

## Decision

Compare each applicable rate/room maximum with the actual party. A difference
between two sufficient maxima is retained as an unexplained source difference,
not an occupancy violation. Missing/invalid limits remain individually UNKNOWN.
Total limits are necessary for this comparison; absent optional adult/child
sublimits are not invented requirements. Present malformed sublimits prevent
full verification. Preserve the shared preparer's conflict/unknown safeguards.

Keep rate assertions, structured mapped inventory, textual concordance and
sleeping sufficiency distinct. Generic titles neither supply sleeping places nor
erase a bound structured inventory. Sofa/bunk places are not inferred; OR never
becomes AND. A sufficient known lower bound may coexist with unquantified extra
places. This is not verified historical allocation or commercial eligibility.

Qualify mapped text in bounded paragraph/sentence clauses, retaining original
fields, exact slices and transformations. Extra-bed/crib conditions, linen and
unrelated facilities do not automatically qualify base beds. Genuine negations,
conditions and unsupported bed statements remain visible and blocking where
their meaning cannot be established. A documented bed count proves neither a
bed type nor places, but can corroborate or contradict structured quantities.
