## Context, decision and rationale

The @1.2 appendix couples all facts to the selected historical rate. Its later
PROPERTY_WIDE rating exception cannot overcome the earlier continuity/receipt
checks. A property-scale proof should establish the source, property and precise
published observation, not commercial verification of a room or tariff.

Version the appendix @1.3 and add dedicated property-rating evidence/review @1.
Derive identity from the validated final REVIEWED fields, their retained proof
references and hashes, packet, journal, projection and original rating claim.
Require a shared original identity/rating proof, not a name/score match. Restrict
this branch to ratingScale PROPERTY_WIDE; mixed-domain use is an input rejection.
Keep all old exact-offer checks for the other fields and existing proof format.

The receipt attests only the property-observation/rule linkage and transformation.
Materialization derives applicability from that linkage, not continuity.basis.
Retain the capture/acquisition clocks and explicit historical-validity limitations;
do not claim source authentication, rate continuity or retrospective custody.

Extract pure preparation shared by the executor. It verifies base and appendix,
retains rejected/insufficient/conflicting additions and prepares the projection,
but cannot call kernel/policy or produce a decision/abstention. Execution remains
a separate explicit callback boundary. No automatic migration of documents.

## Alternatives rejected and safeguards

Rejected: a fake SAME_HISTORICAL_RATE or broad HUMAN receipt; numeric/name-only
identity; dropping all applicability checks; extending property approval to cost,
bookability or accommodation; silently updating historical appendices; using the
executor as a validation-only probe. Expiry, semantic decoding, rating bounds,
conflicts, R1/R2 requirements, source integrity and essential needs remain intact.
The formula observed/max*10, weights, roles and public behavior do not change.
