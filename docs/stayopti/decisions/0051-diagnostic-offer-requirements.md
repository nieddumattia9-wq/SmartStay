## Problem and decision

D-0050 correctly refused unfaithful engine input but left geographic-reference,
availability and sleeping/child obligations unevaluated. Add a forward-only pure
evaluation contract and deterministic evaluators, plus a reviewed-input successor
that reuses all original integrity checks and preserves every observation.
D-0050 and its historical findings are not rewritten.

Source-centre identity is distinct from the selected geographic point. Permit
diagnostic distance comparisons for a documented common source reference without
geographic equivalence, coordinate invention or a policy tolerance. Preserve the
existing strong preference and case-specific exception rules. Observed availability
is not boolean bookability. Known unavailable, unknown and conflicting evidence
remain explicit; ordinary missingness is not low quality.

Evaluate units, guest capacity, documented sleeping places, age admission, pricing
treatment and requested privacy separately. Standard lexical single/double bed
meaning may be normalized transparently; sofa/bunk places, dimensions and exclusive
allocation are not inferred. Adult pricing by age never rewrites family composition.
Property-wide policy is not silently certified as a selected-rate final amount.

## Evidence and safeguards

Every claim retains source-field/proof hashes, exact room/rate/stay/party scope,
applicability and declared observation time. UNKNOWNs, reviewed history and original
scenario are immutable. No additional requirement is inferred from observed rooms.
Supported canonical input is checked and passed unchanged; missing conversion
evidence returns precise blockers. Price incompleteness remains recommendation-
ineligible in the unchanged engine; rating-scale omission remains allowed.

Rejected: relabelling all old blockers without evaluating evidence; source centre
as selected point; forced bookable true/false; guest capacity as bed count; internal
rooms as multiple units; children changed into adults; source-level terms borrowed
from another tariff; a human over-budget role mapped into Upgrade; blind/Golden
promotion from a successful requirements check.

in `docs/engine-v3/d0051-diagnostic-offer-requirements.md`. Private material never
